Live · Community platform

The Conclave

Paid Telegram access, verified through X.

  • Node.js
  • Telegraf
  • Redis
  • X OAuth

The problem

Selling access to a private group meant checking every subscriber by hand.

What I built

Members verify with X and receive a single-use invite that expires in 3 minutes. Lapsed subscribers are removed automatically each day. An admin panel inside Telegram and a 15-level XP system run the community, rescaled across 134 members without losing anyone's rank.

Live · Trading tool

Crypto signal bot

A public track record for every call.

  • Node.js
  • Postgres
  • Solana
  • Telegram

The problem

Signal groups ask for trust but rarely show their results.

What I built

A bot that posts Solana memecoin and exchange signals and records every call, so members see the full history. It alerts when tracked wallets buy or sell, with the amount, market cap and contract address. Built to a client's spec.

Live · Monitoring

gwa-tracker

Instant alerts when 54 accounts post.

  • Python
  • Playwright
  • Fly.io
  • Telegram API

The problem

Spotting posts with certain keywords by scrolling is slow, and being late means missing out.

What I built

A watcher that checks 54 accounts on a schedule and sends subscribers a Telegram alert with a preview. When Cloudflare blocked the first setup, I moved it to Fly.io and split the accounts into batches to keep the session stable. Paid access uses 31-day tokens.

Live · Creator tool

PickMe

Fair giveaways for live streams.

  • Node.js
  • Render
  • Kick
  • Twitch

The problem

Streamers pick giveaway winners by hand, and spam or fake accounts spoil the draw.

What I built

Viewers enter with a keyword in chat and get one entry each, however often they type it. The tool filters out fake accounts, favors people who stay in chat, and shows a live leaderboard on stream.
